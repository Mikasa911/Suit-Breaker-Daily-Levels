Crate Escape Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Crate Escape mobile game ("the game", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Crate Escape is developed by [DEVELOPER NAME]. If you have any questions, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

1. Information the game collects

Game progress stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: levels cleared, stars, Gems and Shards, heroes and trails you own, daily rewards and streaks, and your settings. This stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the game, unless you sign in (see below).

Google sign-in and cloud save (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, we receive your Play Games player ID and display name. Your game progress is then saved to the cloud using Unity Authentication and Unity Cloud Save, linked to an anonymous player ID, so you can restore it on another device. You can play without signing in.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We receive a purchase confirmation and order ID, which we use to deliver what you bought, restore purchases and make sure a purchase is never granted twice.

Gameplay statistics

The game may send anonymous gameplay events through Unity Analytics, such as levels started and completed, hints used, ads watched and items bought, together with an anonymous identifier and basic device information. We use this only to understand how the game is played and to improve it.

Daily puzzle download

To get the daily puzzle, the game downloads a puzzle file from GitHub. No personal information is sent, but, like any website, GitHub receives your IP address as part of the request.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device. They are created on the device itself and no information is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device's settings.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game and save and restore your progress.
  • To deliver and restore purchases.
  • To show ads that keep the game free.
  • To understand how the game is played, fix problems and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The game uses these services, which handle information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob, Google Play Billing and Google Play Games Services: Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Authentication, Cloud Save and Analytics: Unity Privacy Policy
  • GitHub (daily puzzle file): GitHub Privacy Statement

4. Your choices

  • Ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Cloud save: you can sign out, or delete your cloud save, in the game under Settings > Account.
  • Progress on the device: you can erase it in Settings > Reset game data, or by uninstalling the game.
  • Deletion requests: email us at [CONTACT EMAIL] to ask us to delete data linked to your account.

5. Data retention

Cloud progress is kept while your account exists and is deleted when you delete your cloud save or ask us to delete it. Analytics and advertising data are kept by Unity and Google for the periods described in their policies.

6. Children

Crate Escape is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Information sent to and from Google and Unity services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use the protections these services provide.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game gets new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
